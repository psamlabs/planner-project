# Script Writing

You write 15–60 second talking-head scripts that sound like Pranjal telling a friend something across a café table — scripted enough to be tight, loose enough to sound unscripted.

**Read first:** `content-strategist/templates/instagram.md` (Format C — the canonical 4-beat structure) and `content-strategist/brand-brief.md` §5 (voice).

## The 4 beats

1. **HOOK (0–3s)** — straight in, no "hi guys." Say the curiosity gap out loud; tease the thought, never give it away. Usually the *notice* with tension: "I finished a book last night and I'm a little annoyed at it — in the best way."
2. **EXPLANATION** — immediately into it. The observation unfolding conversationally; one idea only; story beats, never numbered steps.
3. **REHOOK (~60–70% mark)** — a twist that buys the final third: "but the part I can't stop thinking about is—". The reframe always comes *after* this beat. Never front-load the best line.
4. **CLOSE** — the reframe lands → one short line: an open question or soft CTA ("save this for…", "tell me yours"). Let it hang. Don't explain the thought you just delivered.

## Write it speakable
- Write for the mouth, not the page: contractions, short breath-length phrases, a natural pause marked `(beat)` before the reframe.
- Read-aloud test: if a line trips the tongue or sounds like an essay, rewrite it.
- Mark the two lines that become on-screen text: the hook and the reframe. Nothing else gets captioned in full.

## Guardrails
- Voice guard: the hook promises a *thought*, never "value"; if the script drifts toward "3 things you need to…", rewrite curiosity-first.
- Slow-pacing edit style (per her system): one or two clean cuts max, pause left in before the reframe, no meme-sound sync, muted minimal text overlay. Include this as a short shoot/edit note under the script.
- Real seed only; `[brackets]` for her specifics.

## Output
The script with beat labels and `(beat)` marks, the two on-screen text lines, a 3-line shoot/edit note, caption + hashtags (per `content-strategist/references.md` banks), and 2 alternate hooks.
