# Month 1 Content Strategy — Summary

*Pranjal Sharma · personal content · Instagram + LinkedIn*

---

## The one thing this is all about

**The promise:** *Whenever Pranjal posts, I leave with one interesting thought I hadn't considered before.*
Books, cafés, campaigns, matcha are just formats. The product is a *shift in how you see something ordinary.*

**The engine (every post):** **Notice → Connect → Reframe.** A small real observation → connected to a far-away idea → delivered as one new thought. This operationalizes Pranjal's edge: *noticing what others overlook and connecting unrelated worlds.*

**Two windows, one brain:**
- **LinkedIn** — *Pranjal the strategist.* Warm but sharp, insight-forward.
- **Instagram** — *Pranjal the curious creative.* Warm, sensory, feeling-forward.
- One idea can live on both — **re-expressed, never copy-pasted.**

**Voice:** curiosity-first (not funny-, emotional-, or educational-first). Curious · warm · thoughtful · playfully witty · reachable. No hustle culture, no listicles, no engagement bait.

**Goal right now:** audience + authority. **Not selling.** Building the runway for the business.

---

## The four recurring series (the feed's backbone)

| Series | Cadence | Format | What it does |
|--------|---------|--------|--------------|
| 🧠 **Notice of the Week** | Weekly | IG carousel + LinkedIn twin | The flagship — the engine in its purest form. The promise, on schedule. |
| 🔖 **Rent-Free** | Weekly | IG quote-card carousel | A line that lives rent-free (a reframe or book quote) + what it dismantled. Builds the reading-club muscle. |
| 📚 **Shelf Check** | Monthly | IG carousel | Her real shelf, *mapped* — series gaps, no-algorithm choosing. Quietly demonstrates the product's worldview. |
| 🔍 **I Spy** | Monthly | IG interactive carousel | "Spot what I noticed" — trains the audience's noticing muscle. Drives comments + saves. |

**Weekly rhythm:** 3 Instagram + 3 LinkedIn. Non-negotiable floor: Notice of the Week + Rent-Free + the LinkedIn twin.

---

## The week-by-week arc

**Week 1 — Establish the rhythm** *(lead: human behaviour)*
Introduce the flagship (matcha → *attention isn't spent, it's what life is made of*) and the two weekly series. A contrarian marketing post, a reading-bridge, and a painting single. **No Stacked yet** — Thursday's Rent-Free ("taste is built, not given") quietly plants the seed the reveal will pay off.

**Week 2 — The reveal** *(lead: marketing)*
Warm up "give-first" trust (café → *your onboarding is a café counter*), then the **Stacked reveal** on LinkedIn as proof-of-work, echoed softly by Rent-Free ("a shelf is a map, not a feed") and *demonstrated* by the first **Shelf Check**.

**Week 3 — Depth** *(lead: books & ideas)*
Go deeper on reading and behaviour (a book that "ruined a belief in the best way"). Stacked's second build-in-public beat: the **design lesson** ("series-gap tracking taught me how people actually finish things"). A work story anchors the authority side.

**Week 4 — Zoom out** *(lead: creativity & storytelling)*
Widen to how ideas travel and how creativity feels. Stacked's third beat: the **position** ("no algorithm isn't a missing feature, it's a stance"). Close the month with the first **I Spy** — the audience's noticing, turned into a game.

## The Stacked build-in-public arc (proof-of-work, never a pitch)

**Stacked** (`stacked.ooam.workers.dev`) — Pranjal's own book-cataloging app. *Your shelf, mapped.* No algorithm; series-gap tracking. A content strategist who *ships her own product* is the strongest possible authority signal.

Content never says "sign up." It makes people *think the way Stacked thinks,* so discovering it feels inevitable:

1. **Wk 1 — Philosophy seeded** (no product mention): taste is built, not fed.
2. **Wk 2 — The reveal**: "I built a book app to fix an itch" + Shelf Check shows it in action.
3. **Wk 3 — The design lesson**: what building series-gap tracking taught about behaviour.
4. **Wk 4 — The position**: "no algorithm" as a deliberate stance, not a gap.

**Rules:** ~1 in 6–8 posts mention Stacked (Week 2 clusters as the reveal exception, then dial back). Always proof/philosophy, never a CTA — link in bio only. Never promote competitor book apps.

---

## How "working" is measured

In priority order: **resonance** (saves, shares, "I never thought of it that way") → **return** (people who always read her) → **right-fit growth** → **authority signals** (collabs, founders in comments) → reach/followers (a *lagging* indicator, never the target).

Every strong reframe gets saved — it becomes the table of contents for Stacked's future newsletter, workshops, and community.
