# Hooks & CTAs — Swipe File

> Curiosity-first hooks and low-pressure invitations, tuned to Pranjal's voice.
> A hook's only job: earn the next line. Never the whole idea, never bait.
> Fill [brackets] with a *real* detail — specificity is what makes a hook stop the scroll.

---

## Curiosity-first hook patterns

### The Noticing (your default — lead with the concrete)
- "The [café / shop / person] near me does one small thing no one else does…"
- "I noticed something on my walk today that I can't stop thinking about."
- "I've done [tiny ritual] the same way for [N] days. Here's why I won't optimise it."
- "Someone said [X] to me offhand last week and it rewired how I see [Y]."

### The Quiet Contradiction
- "[Common belief] is repeated everywhere. It's also where I see the most expensive mistakes."
- "Everyone says [X]. I did [X] for years. I think we've been solving the wrong problem."
- "The advice isn't wrong. It's just describing the seat, not the person."

### The Reading Leak (Pillar 1)
- "I read a line last night I keep applying to [marketing / friendship / attention]."
- "I finished [book] and I'm a little annoyed at it — in the best way."
- "I didn't underline this because it's pretty. I underlined it because it broke a belief."

### The Small Confession (Instagram-leaning, warm)
- "I almost didn't post this because [the honest reason]."
- "I think the least productive thing I do might be the most important."
- "Tell me I'm not the only one who [oddly specific habit]."

### The Overlap tease (your signature)
- "A coffee counter taught me more about onboarding than any deck."
- "A friendship explained attention to me better than any marketing course."
- "[Everyday thing] and [work thing] are secretly the same thing. Let me show you."

### The Work Story open (LinkedIn-leaning)
- "A founder once asked me to make his brand 'sound more premium.' We were both wrong."
- "We rewrote one [subject line / caption] [N] times. The lesson wasn't about words."
- "The most useful thing I learned in [N] years wasn't a strategy. It was watching people."

## Hook anti-patterns (never use)
- "Here are 5 [tips / ways / hacks] to…" — listicle framing
- "Unpopular opinion:" / "Hot take:" / "🚨" — bait
- "Let me tell you a story…" — just start the story
- "Agree? / Thoughts? / Comment below 👇" as the *hook*
- "In today's fast-paced world…" / "We live in a society…" — dead air
- Anything hustle: "grind", "5am", "no excuses", "sigma"

---

## Curiosity-gap discipline (for carousel covers & reel first lines)
The cover shows the *tension*, never the *resolution*.
- ✅ "I've made my matcha the same way for 300 mornings. I think it's the most important thing I do." (why? → swipe)
- ❌ "Slow rituals reduce stress and improve focus." (idea given away → no swipe)

Rule: if someone could screenshot slide 1 and get the whole point, it's not a hook, it's a summary.

## CTA bank — invitations, not extraction

### LinkedIn (make them apply it to their work)
- "Where does this break down for you?"
- "What's the smallest change that made the biggest difference?"
- "What are you reading that keeps leaking into your work?"
- "Curious how this lands for people who [do X]."
- *(or none — a clean reframe can stand alone)*

### Instagram (warmth + belonging)
- "What's your version of this?"
- "Save this for the next time you [forget it]."
- "Tell me I'm not the only one."
- "What are you paying attention to lately — on purpose?"
- *(or just an emoji and silence)*

### Reading-club / community seeds (use sparingly, ~1 in 5 posts)
These quietly build toward the future community/newsletter without selling:
- "Adding this to my growing list of sentences that live rent-free. What's on yours?"
- "I'm collecting these. Send me the one you can't stop thinking about."
- "One day this list becomes something. For now, it's just us noticing together."

## The reframe line — how to sharpen the "one new thought"
The reframe is the post. If it's weak, the post is weak. Sharpen it:
- **Make it a flip:** "It's not X, it's Y." ("Premium isn't polish. It's revealed obsession.")
- **Make it a relocation:** "Your [work thing] is a [everyday thing]." ("Your onboarding is a café counter.")
- **Make it a redefinition:** "[Familiar word] isn't [assumed meaning]. It's [truer meaning]." ("Taste isn't something you have. It's something you build.")
- **Test:** could the reader repeat it to a friend tonight? If not, tighten it.
