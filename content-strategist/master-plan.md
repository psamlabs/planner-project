## The operating rhythm (how the work actually happens)

**~4.5 hrs/week, in fixed blocks — batching beats daily scrambling:**

| Block | When | What |
|-------|------|------|
| **Plan** (30 min) | Sun | Idea-bank sweep: capture the week's real noticings; slot seeds; check next week's ⚠️ fill-ins |
| **Write** (2×45 min) | Mon + Wed eve | Finalise/personalise 3 posts per session from drafts (or persona for new ones) |
| **Shoot** (45 min) | Sat, good light | The week's shot list (each draft file has one) + Pillar-4 stockpile |
| **Design** (45 min) | Sat | Carousel slides from templates (Canva: flagship, Rent-Free, I Spy master files) |
| **Engage** (15 min/day) | after posting | Reply to every real comment in the first hour; save audience language to idea-bank |

**Scheduling:** batch-schedule the week on Sunday (Meta Business Suite / Buffer for IG, LinkedIn native). Post IG at your audience's evening window; LinkedIn mornings. Adjust after Month 1's data — timing folklore matters less than consistency.

**The I Spy album rule:** keep a running phone album of noticed things all month. I Spy dies if you try to reconstruct it at month-end.

## Pre-publish checklist (every post, 60 seconds)

1. ⚠️ **Fill-ins done?** No `[brackets]` left; the observation is *actually true for you*.
2. **Reframe present?** One new thought, repeatable to a friend tonight.
3. **Right window?** LI = strategist; IG = curious creative; not cross-pasted.
4. **Voice check:** curiosity-first; no listicle, hustle, or bait energy.
5. **Stacked check:** if mentioned — proof/philosophy only, no CTA, within cadence cap.
6. **Hashtags:** IG 5–12 from `references.md` + `#thoughtfulcreator`; LI ≤2.
7. **First line test:** does it work with everything after it hidden?

## Checkpoints & decision rules

**Weekly (Fri, 10 min):** log per post — saves, shares, comments-with-thought, any "never thought of it that way." Note the week's best line → `idea-bank.md` greatest hits.

**Day-30 review (45 min) — decides Month 2's flex slots:**
- Rank the month's 24 posts by *resonance* (not reach).
- Which **series** is strongest? Which **collision type** (café×marketing? book×behaviour?) won?
- Reveal-post audit: what did people actually respond to in the Stacked posts? (That's your positioning language — theirs, not yours.)
- Decision rule: **top-2 resonating post types get +1 slot/week in Month 2; the weakest flex slot gets dropped or reinvented.** Series spine stays regardless.

**Day-60 review:** same, plus — is the community flywheel turning (I Spy submissions, Rent-Free contributions, DMs)? If yes → green-light Month 3's club pilot. If no → diagnose (reach problem vs. resonance problem) before adding new surface area.

**Day-90 review:** audience + authority scorecard against `brand-brief.md §10`; decide newsletter/club/hold; write the next 90-day master plan.

## Contingency rules (decided now, so slips don't become spirals)

- **Crunch week:** ship the floor only — Notice of the Week + its LI twin + Rent-Free (3 posts, ~90 min). Everything else skips guilt-free. *The series spine never breaks; the flex slots are the shock absorber.*
- **Missed a week entirely:** don't apologise on-feed, don't double-post to "catch up." Resume with the flagship. (Consistency = returning, not never missing.)
- **Reveal underperforms:** change nothing for 2 weeks. The arc (design lesson, cost post, Shelf Check) *is* the follow-up; proof-of-work compounds slowly. Panic-pivoting to promotion would break the "discovered, not sold" strategy exactly when it matters.
- **A post flops:** log it, find the missing beat (usually: no real noticing, or no reframe), move on. One post is noise; a month is signal.
- **A post takes off:** don't chase it with a copy. Feed its reframe into Rent-Free 2–3 weeks later, and note *why* it worked in the day-30 review.
- **Running dry:** the benches in `idea-bank.md` hold 20+ seeds; the real fix is the Sunday sweep — dryness means noticing lapsed, not ideas.
