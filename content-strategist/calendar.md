# Content Calendar & Cadence

> A rhythm built around your real strengths (writing, carousels, talking to camera, art)
> and the audience+authority goal. Sustainable beats heroic — consistency is the strategy.
> One idea → two windows. Never invent from scratch twice.

---

## The core principle: one idea, two expressions

You don't run two content machines. You have **one brain** that expresses ideas twice.

```
        ONE SEED (a real thing you noticed / read / did)
                          │
        ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
        ▼                                     ▼
   LINKEDIN                               INSTAGRAM
   the strategic version                 the human/sensory version
   (analysis, work, brand)               (feeling, lens, ritual)
```

**Example:** the café "gives the cup first" observation →
- **LinkedIn:** the onboarding/trust essay (Format A).
- **Instagram:** a photo of the café + a quiet caption about giving before asking.

Same insight. Two truths. Zero copy-paste.

## Recommended weekly cadence (built on the four series)

Designed to be *doable*, then scale. Quality of thought > volume. The two weekly series are the non-negotiable spine; everything else flexes.

| Slot | Platform | What | Locked? |
|------|----------|------|---------|
| **Tue** | Instagram | 🧠 **Notice of the Week** (flagship carousel) | ✅ every week |
| **Thu** | Instagram | 🔖 **Rent-Free** (quote-card carousel) | ✅ every week |
| **Sat** | Instagram | Supporting: art single / reel — OR the month's 📚 Shelf Check / 🔍 I Spy | monthly series lands here |
| **Mon** | LinkedIn | The strategic twin of Notice of the Week (re-expressed) | ✅ every week |
| **Wed** | LinkedIn | Strategist post — Work Story / Contrarian / **Stacked build-in-public** | ✅ every week |
| **Fri** | LinkedIn | Reading-bridge (Pillar 1→5) or campaign teardown | flex |

**Weekly total:** 3 Instagram + 3 LinkedIn. If capacity is tight, the floor is **Notice of the Week + Rent-Free + its LinkedIn twin** (never drop those). Never miss two weeks in a row — return matters more than frequency.

### Where the monthly series & Stacked arc land
- **Shelf Check** → Week 2 Saturday. **I Spy** → Week 4 Saturday.
- **Stacked build-in-public arc** (proof-of-work, never a pitch — see brand-brief §8):
  - Wk 1: seed the *philosophy* (shelves as maps) — no product mention yet.
  - Wk 2: the **reveal** ("I built a book app — here's the itch") + Shelf Check shows it in action.
  - Wk 3: a **design/behaviour lesson** from building it (series-gap tracking, no-algorithm as a stance).
  - Wk 4: **why no-algorithm is a position, not a feature** — the thinking behind the product.

## The weekly ritual (how to actually run this — ~90 min/week)

1. **Sunday — Collect (20 min):** open `idea-bank.md`. Look back at your week. What did you *actually* notice, read, make, or hear? Add 3–5 real seeds. (Your edge is noticing — this is the most important step.)
2. **Sunday — Assign (10 min):** slot this week's seeds into the platforms/formats above.
3. **Mid-week — Write (2× 25 min):** hand seeds to the strategist persona (`strategist.md`), edit for your truth.
4. **Batch-shoot art/photos when the light's good** (Pillar 4 stockpile — never scramble).
5. **Friday — Review (10 min):** what got saved/shared? What made someone say "I never thought of that"? Note it.

---

## Monthly review (30 min — this is where authority compounds)

Ask, in order:
1. **Which posts kept the promise best?** (Saves, shares, "I never thought of it that way.") Do more of *that connection style.*
2. **Which pillar over/under-performed** — for *resonance*, not just reach?
3. **What language did the audience use** about you / back to you? (Future product & newsletter copy lives here.)
4. **What's one experiment for next month?** (New format, a further-apart connection, first newsletter tease.)
5. **Is it still one brain?** Do LinkedIn and Instagram feel like the same person in two windows?

> Reminder from the brief: reach is a *lagging* indicator. Optimize resonance; growth follows trust.

## Repurposing map (squeeze more from one seed)
- A strong LinkedIn post → carousel (slow it down, make it sensory) → reel (say the reframe to camera).
- A book quote → IG quote card → LinkedIn reading-bridge → newsletter section (later).
- A painting → IG single → a Pillar 2 "how creativity feels" carousel → a workshop idea (much later).
- **Save every "one new thought" reframe in the idea bank.** Your best lines become your future digital product's table of contents.
