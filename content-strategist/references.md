### The one that matters most for the long game
**@swipethebook → @weare.booked.** They built a *community around reading* — which is the exact muscle you'll need for Stacked's reading-club / newsletter. Study the *mechanics*: how they invite, how they run a shared read, how membership feels. **Borrow the community playbook, not a product pitch** — you're still in the "don't sell yet" phase (`brand-brief.md §8`).

### Skip as inspiration (named so you know why)
- **@hartimesocial** and **@writewithcarla** — "tips / hacks / services" accounts. This is exactly the **listicle, hustle-adjacent framing your brand-brief tells you to avoid** (§5 Don'ts). Glance at them only to see what the *crowded, generic version* of this space looks like — so you can be the opposite. Never a voice model.

### How to study without drifting
- **Aesthetic & format** → @showandtellbooknook, @priyaannshuu, @thebrandbrew_.
- **Community mechanics** → @swipethebook / @weare.booked.
- **Marketing currency** → @gradgirlmarketing (read to *know what's happening*, then react to it in *your* reflective voice).
- **Voice** → still Tarini + reflective Substack writers + thoughtful carousel creators, but more playful and social (`brand-brief.md §5`). None of the accounts above override that.

> Watch-out: the 286K and 57.4K accounts have gravity. It's easy to unconsciously adopt trend-news punchiness or agency polish. If a draft starts sounding like a trend recap or a pitch deck, pull it back to curiosity-first.

## Hashtag & keyword banks

**First principle (from `hooks-and-ctas.md`):** the **cover slide / first line does ~90% of discovery.** Tags are a small nudge, not the engine. Use a *specific, on-brand* handful — never 30 generic ones.

### 📚 Books
`#bookstagram` · `#bookrecs` · `#ilovebooks` · `#bookishlife` · `#reading`
→ **plus genre-specific tags matched to the actual book** (`#romancebooks`, `#fantasybooks`, etc.). Match to the real title — don't spray genres.

### 🎬💼 Marketing & creativity
Keep it **light** — hack-y marketing tags skew hustle-culture and pull the wrong crowd.
`#brandstorytelling` · `#contentstrategist`
→ That's plenty. The hook carries this side; tags won't.

### ☕ Aesthetic & lifestyle
`#shelfie` · `#homelibrary` · `#slowliving` · `#cozyaesthetic`

### 🎯 The precision tag (use most posts)
`#thoughtfulcreator` — **low volume, but precise and on-brand.** It signals to the *right small audience* rather than chasing the algorithm. Worth including even though traffic is low; it's a flag for your people, not a reach play.

### Sane defaults per post
- Pick **5–12** tags, weighted to the post's actual pillar.
- Always include `#thoughtfulcreator` + 1–2 niche tags over broad ones.
- A book post: 4–5 book tags + genre-match + `#thoughtfulcreator`.
- A shelf/lifestyle post: `#shelfie #homelibrary #slowliving #cozyaesthetic #thoughtfulcreator`.
- A marketing/creativity post: `#brandstorytelling #contentstrategist #thoughtfulcreator` — and let the hook do the work.
