*(Not reviews. The question it left you with.)*

- A book you're "annoyed at in the best way" — the idea you can't shake.
- A Substack essay about [X] that secretly explained [unrelated Y] to you.
- A film/show whose *structure* taught you something about how ideas travel.
- "Sentences that live rent-free" — a running series (community seed).

- The idea a book gave you → translated into how people build taste / pay attention / trust.

## 🎬 Pillar 2 — Creativity & storytelling
- Why a specific campaign *stayed with you* — the mechanism, not the praise.
- "What I learned about AI this week" — through a creativity lens, not a tutorial.
- How creativity actually *feels* on a hard day vs. an easy one.
- Your painting/cooking process as a metaphor for making anything.
- Why some ideas get shared and near-identical ones don't.
- The difference between clever and memorable (and why brands chase the wrong one).
- A story structure hiding inside an everyday thing (a menu, a queue, a text thread).

## 🧠 Pillar 3 — Human behaviour
- The un-optimisable two minutes (matcha) → attention isn't spent, it's what life is made of.
- Why we save things we never revisit — the psychology of "future me."
- How friendships are really an attention economy.
- Taste as something you build, not something you have.
- Why "for here or to go?" *after* the cup builds trust — sequencing & commitment.
- The thing people are quietly trying to feel vs. what they say they want.
- Growing up = your taste and your fears quietly swapping places.
- Why we trust specific over impressive (obsession over polish).

- A beginner-friendly art moment — inviting the shy makers in.
- Your desk / workspace + what the mess or order says about how you think.

## 💼 Pillar 5 — Marketing

- A campaign across [fashion/crypto/hospitality] and the *human* thing underneath it.
- What founders get wrong about "brand" vs. what actually earns trust.
