# Pranjal's Content Strategist 🧠✨

A reusable content system for building an engaged, curious audience on **LinkedIn** and
**Instagram** — the runway for a future creator-led business that sells *thinking, not time.*

Built around one promise:
> **Whenever Pranjal posts, I leave with one interesting thought I hadn't considered before.**

---

## The idea in 30 seconds

- **One brain, two windows.** LinkedIn = *Pranjal the strategist.* Instagram = *Pranjal the curious creative.* Same person, re-expressed — never copy-pasted.
- **One engine.** Every post runs **Notice → Connect → Reframe**: a real observation, connected to a far-away idea, delivered as one new thought.
- **One moat.** You notice what others overlook and connect unrelated worlds until something familiar looks new. This system makes that repeatable instead of accidental.

---

## The files

| File | What it's for | When you open it |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| **`brand-brief.md`** | The source of truth: promise, edge, engine, voice, pillars, audience, metrics. | Read first. Re-read monthly. Settle any "should I post this?" question here. |
| **`strategist.md`** | A paste-in persona that turns any AI chat into your on-brand ghostwriter. | Every time you write a post. |
| **`templates/linkedin.md`** | 4 LinkedIn formats + worked examples in your voice. | Writing for LinkedIn. |
| **`templates/instagram.md`** | 4 Instagram formats (carousel/caption/reel/quote) + examples. | Writing for Instagram. |
| **`templates/hooks-and-ctas.md`** | Swipe file: curiosity-first hooks, CTAs, reframe sharpeners. | When the hook or ending isn't landing. |
| **`calendar.md`** | Weekly cadence, pillar rotation, a seeded 4-week calendar, review rituals. | Sunday planning + monthly review. |
| **`idea-bank.md`** | A running well of seeds mapped to your 5 pillars + your "greatest hits" reframes. | Whenever you notice something. Constantly. |

---

## How to run it each week (~90 min total)

1. **Sunday — collect & plan (30 min):** open `idea-bank.md`, add the real things you noticed this week, and slot them into `calendar.md`.
2. **Mid-week — write (2× 25 min):** paste the persona from `strategist.md` into a fresh chat, hand it a seed + platform, edit the output for your truth.
3. **Ongoing — shoot art/photos when the light's good** (Pillar 4 stockpile).
4. **Friday — note what resonated** (saves, shares, "I never thought of that"). Add winning reframes to the idea bank.
5. **Monthly — review** (`calendar.md`) and do more of whatever kept the promise best.

---

## The rules that keep it *you* (not generic AI content)

- **Curiosity-first** — not funny-first, emotional-first, or educational-first.
- **Always start from a real noticing.** The engine needs a true observation to connect from.
- **Every post earns its reframe** — no "one new thought," no post.
- **No hustle culture, no listicles, no engagement bait.** Your audience actively rejects them.
- **Resonance over reach.** Reach is a lagging indicator of trust. Optimize the trust.
